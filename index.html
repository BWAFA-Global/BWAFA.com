The gap we close

By 2030, four in ten of the world's young people will be African. Working-age population, largest on earth by 2040. Nobody builds like this by accident, and the Tribe is proof: real products, real revenue, real culture that travels further than the credit it gets.

Here's the part that stings. Plenty of founders losing rounds right now are objectively better than the ones winning them. Better product, better margins, better founder, told in a deck that undersells all of it, in a contract nobody stress-tested, in a brand voice that only says what they think an investor wants to hear.

That gap is not just about funding, even if funding is where it hurts loudest. It shows up in daily communications that quiet your authority, grant proposals that read generic, and contract clauses no one caught until they cost something. BWAFA closes that gap wherever it appears. We ensure what you have actually built on the ground and what the room in front of you understands are finally the same thing: defensible positioning that competitors cannot easily copy, built on what is real rather than a performance.

What we don't do

Our job is making sure whatever ends up in that filing actually protects what's yours.

What You’re Really Asking

How much does this cost?

The Movement side costs nothing. The Tribe, the online Roundtables, the DMs, the free resource sheets, all of it’s open. The Firm side is a paid engagement, every time. Reach out, we scope where your business actually is, then send terms before anything starts.

What if I can’t afford the Firm right now?

Start in the Movement. Show up, learn, build the relationship. Plenty of people in the Tribe joined with nothing more than curiosity and walked away with a pricing fix or a contract catch that changed their whole trajectory. The paid work comes when you’re ready for it, not before.

Do I need my numbers together before I reach out?

No. Half the founders who need BWAFA most show up with a deck held together by hope and a Canva template. Send whatever you’ve got, messy is fine, that’s the actual starting point of the work, not a reason to wait.

Will BWAFA take equity in my business?

No. You pay for the work, you keep the ownership. If a firm structures your raise and then asks for a slice too, that’s not advisory but a second investor wearing a consultant’s badge.

What if my business is really small right now?

Small isn’t the disqualifier, unclear is. What matters is whether there’s something true underneath the presentation. If there is, we find it and make it legible. If there isn’t yet, we’ll tell you that too.

Can BWAFA file my trademark, register my company, or act as my legal counsel?

No, and we’ll say that plainly every time it comes up. We review, we draft, we restructure. Filing a document with a government registry or standing in as your registered legal counsel belongs to a licensed lawyer in your own country. What we make sure of is that whatever ends up in that filing already protects what’s yours, and we’ll point you to the right person if you don’t have one yet.

I’m not in Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, or Rwanda. Can BWAFA still work with me?

Yes. Those four show up most in the work so far because that’s where the Tribe’s been loudest, not because the door’s shut anywhere else. If you’re building on the continent or in the diaspora and the story isn’t landing the way the business deserves, that’s the actual criteria. The Tribe was never a country, it’s a continent and everyone still building toward it from outside or within. The specific paths, loans, grants, registries, differ by country, the structural work doesn’t.

Do you guarantee I’ll get funded?

No, and anyone who promises that is selling you something else. What we guarantee is that your deck, your model, and your contracts stop being the reason a serious investor says no. What happens after that is between you and the market.

Is BWAFA a funding or grant agency?

No. Funding is usually what happens after the real work, not the work itself. We fix what’s underneath, the deck, the numbers, the terms, so that when capital does show up, it holds.

I don’t need funding right now, do I still need BWAFA?

Probably, and this is the part people miss. A contract nobody stress-tested, a pricing model quietly undercharging you, a brand voice saying what you think people want to hear instead of what’s true, none of that requires a raise to be a problem. It usually just gets found the hard way if it’s ignored long enough.

How long does an engagement actually take?

Depends on what’s broken. A pricing fix might be one sharp session. A full commercial structure audit takes longer, because rushing it defeats the purpose. No timeline promise before the actual problem’s been looked at.

What happens to my information once I send over a deck or contract?

It stays confidential, full stop. Nothing gets touched, shared, or featured without your own sign-off. The Receipts Room only shows people who explicitly agreed to have their story told.

What actually happens after I reach out?

A short scoping conversation first, no pitch, no pressure, just figuring out where it’s actually bleeding. From there, if it’s a fit, we send terms and get to work. If it’s not the right time, we’ll tell you that too.

Do you host physical gatherings?

Yes!! The first one lands November 2026, an intentionally curated gathering called the Returner’s Roundtable. It’s paid, and once you’re in the room, you’ll understand why we didn’t make it free. This isn’t a room full of people collecting business cards, it’s built for people who show up already building, already serious, already done waiting for permission. Free gets attention. This gets outcomes. That’s why there’s a price on the door. Keep an eye on @bwafaglobal and the bio, seats won’t sit open long.

I’m just here for the content, not looking for advisory right now, is that fine?

More than fine. That’s the Movement. Read, engage, show up at a Roundtable. Nobody’s pushed toward a paid engagement for existing in the Tribe. The Firm is there when you’re ready for it, not before.

What actually makes BWAFA different from a regular consultant?

Most consultants show up after the culture’s already been flattened into a template. BWAFA starts from what’s actually true about the business and builds from there, drafting the terms and structure that survive contact with a real investor, not just the pitch.

What does BWAFA actually get out of all this?

A continent that stops losing rounds it should be winning. Every founder who leaves with a deck that holds and a contract that doesn’t get contested raises the bar for the next one behind them. That compounding is the entire business model.
